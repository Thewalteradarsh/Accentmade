Legal & Privacy Information

Notice under Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023) & UK/UAE GDPR

Accent Made values your personal sovereignty. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why we need it, and how you retain full control over your data.

1. Data Fiduciary & Controller Identity

The Data Fiduciary (Controller) is Accent Made, operated as an accredited educational practice by Carys George (CELTA, TEFL, MA, BA Hons).

2. Personal Data We Collect

We process only data strictly necessary to provide customized language instruction:

  • Contact Coordinates: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp/Phone Number.
  • Academic Diagnostics: Self-selected English level, placement check responses, phonetics audio notes, and learning objectives (e.g. IELTS, British accent reduction, Corporate English).
  • Schedule Preferences: Requested weekly class frequency and group vs. private tuition preferences.
  • Zero Financial or Payment Data: No payment card numbers, CVVs, UPI IDs, or banking credentials are collected on this website. Tuition arrangements are coordinated directly between the student and trainer without online gateway storage.

3. Specified Purpose of Processing

Personal data is collected and processed solely for:

  • Evaluating student proficiency and assigning suitable cohort placement (Group, Semi-Private, or 1-on-1).
  • Delivering requested digital study materials (including the complimentary Accent & Fluency Blueprint E-Guide).
  • Communicating class schedules, Zoom/Google Meet links, and diagnostic homework feedback over email or WhatsApp.

Your data is never sold, leased, rented, or shared with third-party data brokers or marketing affiliates.

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the absolute right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting past lawful processing.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct access to our designated Grievance Officer with guaranteed timely resolution.

Terms of Service & Class Participation Policy

Guidelines governing course registration, consultations, class attendance, and curriculum copyright.

1. Course Inquiries & Consultations

Course inquiries, placement consultations, and initial proficiency evaluations are informational and intended to assess student goals. Final enrollment, cohort placement, and class scheduling are confirmed following mutual evaluation and availability review with Carys George.

2. Attendance, Rescheduling & 24-Hour Notice Rule

To maintain fair scheduling for all learners and the trainer:

  • Private 1-on-1 Sessions: Must be rescheduled with at least 24 hours advance written notice via email or WhatsApp. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are considered forfeited.
  • Semi-Private (Trio) Classes: Rescheduling requires unanimous consent of all 3 student members and trainer with 24 hours advance notice.
  • Group Cohorts (6 max): Group classes operate on predetermined weekly schedules and cannot be individually rescheduled. Comprehensive lesson summaries and practice homework will be provided for any missed session.
