ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Meet Carys George

Founder | Communication & Accent Coach | CELTA-Qualified English Trainer

Carys George
Cambridge CELTA • Native UK English Trainer

Accent Made was founded by Carys George, a British English trainer with more than a decade of experience helping people communicate confidently across cultures.

Carys has worked with learners and professionals from a wide range of nationalities and backgrounds, including extensive experience in the UAE, UK and Ireland.

She spent over five years teaching in Dubai, where she had the privilege of working with members of the UAE military, police and navy. Her extensive teaching experience allows her to deliver tailored lessons across all key language skills, including speaking, listening, reading and writing, as well as pronunciation and accent reduction.

Her career has taken her from classrooms to corporate environments, from working with international professionals to supporting students developing English as an additional language.

Today, based in Kerala, Carys combines her international teaching experience with a deep understanding of the challenges faced by Indian professionals communicating in English on a global stage.

A different approach to English training

Carys believes that learning English isn't simply about memorising vocabulary or completing grammar exercises.

It's about becoming comfortable enough to actually use the language.

Her approach combines:

Pronunciation + Communication + Confidence + Personality

Rather than trying to erase someone's identity or make everyone sound the same, Accent Made focuses on clear, confident and effective communication.

Legal & Privacy Information

Notice under Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023) & UK/UAE GDPR

Accent Made values your personal sovereignty. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why we need it, and how you retain full control over your data.

1. Data Fiduciary & Controller Identity

The Data Fiduciary (Controller) is Accent Made, operated as an accredited educational practice by Carys George (CELTA, TEFL, MA, BA Hons).

2. Personal Data We Collect

We process only data strictly necessary to provide customized language instruction:

  • Contact Coordinates: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp/Phone Number.
  • Academic Diagnostics: Self-selected English level, placement check responses, phonetics audio notes, and learning objectives (e.g. IELTS, British accent reduction, Corporate English).
  • Schedule Preferences: Requested weekly class frequency and group vs. private tuition preferences.
  • Zero Financial or Payment Data: No payment card numbers, CVVs, UPI IDs, or banking credentials are collected on this website. Tuition arrangements are coordinated directly between the student and trainer without online gateway storage.

3. Specified Purpose of Processing

Personal data is collected and processed solely for:

  • Evaluating student proficiency and assigning suitable cohort placement (Group, Semi-Private, or 1-on-1).
  • Delivering requested digital study materials (including the complimentary Accent & Fluency Blueprint E-Guide).
  • Communicating class schedules, Zoom/Google Meet links, and diagnostic homework feedback over email or WhatsApp.

Your data is never sold, leased, rented, or shared with third-party data brokers or marketing affiliates.

Under Section 6(1) and Sections 11–14 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, UK GDPR Articles 15–22, and UAE Data Protection Law, you enjoy the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a full summary of personal data currently processed by Accent Made.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate details or demand immediate deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") of all your information.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the absolute right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting past lawful processing.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct access to our designated Grievance Officer with guaranteed timely resolution.
How to Withdraw Consent or Delete Your Records:
Email our dedicated Data Protection & Grievance desk at contact@accentmade.com with the subject line "DPDP / GDPR Data Erasure Request". We will process your erasure across all email and WhatsApp registers within our mandatory 72-hour turnaround window.

Terms of Service & Class Participation Policy

Guidelines governing course registration, consultations, class attendance, and curriculum copyright.

1. Course Inquiries & Consultations

Course inquiries, placement consultations, and initial proficiency evaluations are informational and intended to assess student goals. Final enrollment, cohort placement, and class scheduling are confirmed following mutual evaluation and availability review with Carys George.

2. Attendance, Rescheduling & 24-Hour Notice Rule

To maintain fair scheduling for all learners and the trainer:

  • Private 1-on-1 Sessions: Must be rescheduled with at least 24 hours advance written notice via email or WhatsApp. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are considered forfeited.
  • Semi-Private (Trio) Classes: Rescheduling requires unanimous consent of all 3 student members and trainer with 24 hours advance notice.
  • Group Cohorts (6 max): Group classes operate on predetermined weekly schedules and cannot be individually rescheduled. Comprehensive lesson summaries and practice homework will be provided for any missed session.

3. Intellectual Property & E-Guide Copyright

All curriculum frameworks, phonetic audio demonstrations, diagnostic checklists, and the British Accent & Fluency Blueprint E-Guide are the exclusive intellectual property of Carys George (Accent Made). Downloading or receiving materials grants you an individual, non-transferable license for personal self-study only. Commercial reproduction, redistribution, or public upload is strictly prohibited.

4. Community Code of Conduct

Accent Made welcomes an international cohort of adult learners. Respectful, professional, and courteous decorum is required across all live classrooms, discussion sessions, and cohort WhatsApp groups. Any form of harassment, discrimination, or disruptive conduct may result in immediate termination of tuition without refund.
